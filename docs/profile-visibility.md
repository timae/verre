# Profile visibility

You get to choose who can see your profile. Set it once in your account settings and forget about it.

## The four levels

In **Settings → Profile visibility**, pick one:

- **Everyone** — anyone with the link, search engines included.
- **Verre users** — anyone logged into a Verre account.
- **My followers** — only people who follow you. They don't need to follow back.
- **Mutual follows** — only people you follow back.

Underneath those, there's a **Friends of friends** toggle for the two strictest options. Flip it on if you also want to let in someone one hop away — a friend of a follower, or a friend of a mutual.

## Who sees your posts

In the app, **Settings → Privacy → Post Visibility** decides who sees your posts: your check-ins and your moment posts, in feeds and on your profile. **Same as Account** is on by default: whoever can see your profile sees your posts. Turn it off and pick a smaller audience under **Visible To**:

- **Verre Users**, **Followers** or **Friends**: a smaller audience for your posts only. Followers of followers or friends of friends never apply here.
- **Only Me**: a private diary. Your posts show only to you.

The stricter setting always wins, so a choice wider than your account visibility is greyed out until you change your account visibility, and the same choice as your account visibility is simply **Same as Account** (unless you include friends of friends or followers of followers there: then picking the same choice for posts leaves them out). If you narrow your account visibility past your posts choice, **Same as Account** turns back on.

When your posts are hidden from someone who can see your profile, the app shows them a short line instead, for example "Follow Lena to see posts" (the website shows no posts).

Your settings cover your own posts. Someone else's post (one that tags you, or their post from a moment you were both in) follows their settings. Inside a moment, everyone in it sees everyone's ratings, whatever these settings say.

## On your profile

Also in **Settings → Privacy → On My Profile**:

- **Show Cravings**: others see your Cravings, or not.
- **Show Moments**: others see the moments you joined, or not.
- **Show My Locations**: others see the place (venue, city, country) on your posts, or not. Where a wine comes from always shows. A moment's address stays visible to the people in it.

Badges others see on your profile never show the date you earned them.

## What everyone always sees

Even at the strictest setting, two things stay public so people can still find you and ask to follow:

- Your display name.
- The fact that you exist.

Your avatar is part of the gated content — it only shows to people who pass your tier.

## Defaults

New accounts land on **Verre users**. Logged-in people can see your profile; search engines can't.

## Changing it

Go to **Settings → Profile visibility** (the web) or **Settings → Privacy** (the app; the check-in and On My Profile settings are app-only for now). New views apply right away; people already looking at your profile might see a stale version for about half a minute. If your settings changed somewhere else while you were saving, nothing is overwritten: you see what is saved now and can choose again.
