# Profile visibility

You get to choose who can see your profile. Set it once in your account settings and forget about it.

## The four levels

In **Settings → Profile visibility**, pick one:

- **Everyone** — anyone with the link, search engines included.
- **Verre users** — anyone logged into a Verre account.
- **My followers** — only people who follow you. They don't need to follow back.
- **Mutual follows** — only people you follow back.

Underneath those, there's a **Friends of friends** toggle for the two strictest options. Flip it on if you also want to let in someone one hop away — a friend of a follower, or a friend of a mutual.

## What everyone always sees

Even at the strictest setting, two things stay public so people can still find you and ask to follow:

- Your display name.
- The fact that you exist.

Your avatar is part of the gated content — it only shows to people who pass your tier.

## Defaults

New accounts land on **Verre users**. Logged-in people can see your profile; search engines can't.

## Changing it

Go to **Settings → Profile visibility**. New views apply right away; people already looking at your profile might see a stale version for about half a minute.
