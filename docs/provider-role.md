# Provider role

A lighter-weight session role for someone bringing wines to a tasting without full host powers.

## What providers can do

- **Add wines** to the lineup, exactly like the host can.
- **Edit or delete** only the wines they themselves added.
- See their own wines un-redacted in a blind tasting, even though everyone else's wines stay hidden until the host reveals them.

## What providers cannot do

- Touch other people's wines.
- Change session settings, rename the session, delete the session.
- Reorder the lineup or reveal/hide blind wines (those stay host-only).
- Kick, ban, or change anyone else's role.

## Who can assign the role

In the participants list, tap **Set role** (or **Change role**) on a row. You'll see a picker with the roles that are available to set.

- **Hosts** can assign all three: Co-host, Provider, or Taster.
- **Co-hosts** can only assign Provider or Taster — promoting someone to co-host requires the original host.

A participant can be exactly one of: host, co-host, provider, taster. Picking a new role replaces the old one.

## Blind tasting and providers

In a blind tasting, everyone except the host sees wines as anonymous bottles ("Wine 1", "Wine 2", etc.) until the host taps **reveal**. Providers are a small exception: the wines they added show up un-redacted in their own view, because they brought them and obviously already know what's in the bottle. They still can't see the other tasters' or the host's contributions until those are revealed.

## Kicking or banning a provider

Same as any other participant. Hosts and co-hosts can kick or ban a provider; the wines they added get the same "keep or remove" choice in the moderation modal. The wine's "added by" record stays on the wine, so even after a removal the host can still tell who brought what.
