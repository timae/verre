# Mute

Mute is for when you'd rather not see someone's posts but don't want to make a thing of it.

## What it does

Their check-ins and badge unlocks stop showing in your feed. That's the whole effect.

## What it doesn't do

Pretty much everything else:

- **They don't know.** Their feed, search results, your profile, the sessions you share — all unchanged on their end.
- **You stay following them.** Mute and follow are separate.
- **Their profile still works.** Open `/u/<id>` and you'll see everything as usual.
- **Tags still work.** You can tag them, they can tag you.
- **Sessions are unaffected.** If you're in a tasting together, their ratings and presence show up like normal.

## Muting someone

Open their profile, tap the **⋯** menu next to the follow button, and pick **Mute**. The menu confirms briefly, then closes.

## Unmuting

Same menu — the label flips to **Unmute** while a mute is active.

## Mute vs block

Mute is the quiet option. The other person doesn't know, sessions stay normal, your follow stays intact. You just don't see their posts.

If you want something harder — both of you invisible to each other across profiles, search, likes, follower counts — that's a [Block](./block.md).
